About

A free tool for fantasy managers who stream defenses.

Fantasy Defense Assist started as a personal cheat sheet. Every week I'd open three or four tabs to figure out which defense to pick up, and I wanted one page that answered the question at a glance: which defenses have good matchups this week, which ones are worth holding, and which ones are still on the waiver wire in my league.

What the site does

  • Shows every NFL defense's remaining schedule on one grid, with each matchup colored from tough (red) to great (green).
  • Scores defenses with ESPN, Yahoo or Sleeper default rules, because the platforms score defenses differently.
  • Lets you mark defenses as taken in your league and suggests the best available options each week.
  • Ranks each defense's rest-of-season and fantasy playoff schedule so you can plan a stash.

How it's built

The data comes from public NFL box scores and play-by-play, updated automatically three mornings a week during the season. All scoring happens in your browser. There are no accounts, and your settings stay on your device. You can read exactly how the numbers are calculated in How our rankings work.

Who's behind it

Fantasy Defense Assist is an independent project built by a longtime fantasy football player in Arizona. It isn't affiliated with the NFL, ESPN, Yahoo or Sleeper; team names are used only to identify matchups. The same builder makes Fantasy Injury Assist, which sends injury and status alerts for your roster, and Fantasy League History Assist, which compiles your league's all-time records.
