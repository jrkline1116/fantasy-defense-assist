Planning Your Fantasy Playoff Defense (Weeks 15–17)

Updated October 14, 2026 · 4 min read

Most fantasy leagues hold their playoffs in Weeks 15, 16 and 17. By then, nearly every manager is streaming defenses, and the good matchups get claimed quickly. Planning ahead and stashing a defense with a soft playoff schedule is one of the easiest edges you can find late in the season.

Use the Playoffs view

On the grid, the Rest of season column ranks every defense by how easy its remaining schedule is, using how many points D/STs have scored against each upcoming opponent. Flip its switch to Playoffs and it only counts Weeks 15–17. Click the column header to sort, and the defenses with the friendliest playoff schedules move to the top. A cell like "3rd · 7.4 · 3g" means the third-easiest playoff schedule, with opponents allowing 7.4 points per game to D/STs across three games.

When to stash

  • Around Week 10 to 12 is usually the sweet spot. Schedules are known, offenses have enough games for the ranks to mean something, and most managers aren't thinking about the playoffs yet.
  • Only stash if you have the bench room. If holding a defense means dropping a useful player, wait a couple more weeks.
  • Mind the trade deadline and injuries. A playoff opponent that loses its starting quarterback can turn a decent matchup into a great one, and the reverse is true too.

Look for two out of three

You rarely find a defense with three great playoff matchups. Two good ones is a win, because you can stream for the third week. Check whether a defense has a bye during Weeks 15–17. The column shows how many playoff games each defense has, so a "2g" means it's off one of those weeks and you'll need a fill-in.

Don't forget the regular season

A stash only matters if you make the playoffs. If you're fighting for a spot, keep streaming the best weekly matchup and only hold a playoff defense if it doesn't cost you this week. Our weekly streaming guide covers that routine.

Fantasy finals in Week 18?

Some leagues run their championship in Week 18. Week 18 schedules can be unpredictable, since teams that have clinched sometimes rest starters. If your league plays in Week 18, look at that column on the grid separately and check the news closer to the game.
