Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Fantasy Defense Assist (fantasydefenseassist.com) is a free fantasy football tool. This page explains what information is involved when you use it.

No accounts, no personal information

You don't need an account to use the site, and we don't ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information.

Settings saved in your browser

Your choices, such as which scoring tab you picked and which defenses you marked as taken, are saved in your own browser's local storage so they're still there next time. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Advertising and cookies

This site may show ads served by Google. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Services that load with the page

Like most websites, loading this site involves a few outside services, which receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site
  • Google Fonts, which provides the typefaces
  • ESPN's image servers, which provide the team logos

Each of these services handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be posted on the site's GitHub page.
