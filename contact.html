Contact

Questions, bug reports and ideas are all welcome.

Email: support@fantasydefenseassist.com

I read every message and usually reply within two or three days, sooner during the season.

Helpful details to include

  • A number that doesn't match your league: the defense, the week, which tab you were on (ESPN, Yahoo or Sleeper), and the number your league shows. A screenshot helps.
  • Something not working: what you clicked, what happened, and your device and browser (for example, iPhone with Safari).
  • Feature ideas: what you'd like the site to help you decide.

Other ways to reach us

Technical issues can also be reported on the project's GitHub page. For how we handle data and advertising, see the privacy policy.
